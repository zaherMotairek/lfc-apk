1. WHAT IS THE KOP PLATFORM?
================================================================================

The Kop Platform is the official digital home of OLSC Lebanon (Official
Liverpool Supporters Club — Lebanon branch).

It is NOT a replacement for official OLSC membership. Official membership
is still managed by the committee. The platform gives approved members a
modern digital experience:

  • Match Hub — predict scores, compete on the leaderboard
  • Live GOOOOAAAAAL alerts during matches
  • Digital membership card with QR code
  • Upload photo of existing physical OLSC card
  • Community feed, news, events, gallery
  • Hall of Fame and gamification (XP, levels, achievements)

The public website (olsclebanon.com or localhost in development) is open
to everyone. The Member App and Admin Panel require login.

4. MEMBERSHIP — HOW IT WORKS (STEP BY STEP)
================================================================================

IMPORTANT: Official OLSC membership is managed by the committee outside the
platform. The platform does NOT sell or approve membership on its own.

─── STEP 1: Committee approves a member offline ───
The person becomes an official OLSC member through the normal committee
process and receives an Official Member ID.

─── STEP 2: Admin creates the member on the platform ───
Go to: Admin Panel → Members → Create member

Fill in:
  • First name, last name, email
  • Official Member ID (from the committee — must be unique)
  • Season label (e.g. "2026-27")
  • Phone (optional)

Click "Create & send invitation".

─── STEP 3: Member receives invitation ───
If email (SMTP) is configured: member gets an email with activation link.
If email is NOT configured (development): the invitation link/token appears
in the admin response — copy it and send manually to the member.

Invitation link format:
  https://yoursite.com/accept-invitation?token=XXXXX

Invitation expires after 72 hours (default).

─── STEP 4: Member activates account ───
Member opens the link, sets a password, and account becomes ACTIVE.

─── STEP 5: Digital membership card is auto-generated ───
Card number format: OLSC-{season}-{officialMemberId}
Example: OLSC-2026-27-12345

Member can view card at: Member App → Digital Membership Card
  • QR code for event check-in
  • Name, official ID, season

─── STEP 6 (optional): Upload physical card photo ───
Members who already have a printed OLSC card can upload a photo:
  Member App → Digital Membership Card → Upload card photo
Supported: JPEG, PNG, WebP (max 5 MB)

─── STEP 7: Event verification ───
At watch parties, committee staff with EVENT_MANAGER role can scan the
member's QR code to verify: VALID, EXPIRED, REVOKED, or NOT FOUND.

8. MATCH HUB — COMPLETE WORKFLOW
================================================================================

This is one of the most important admin workflows.

─── PHASE 1: CREATE THE MATCH ───
Admin → Matches → Create match

Fill in:
  • Opponent (e.g. "Arsenal")
  • Competition (e.g. "Premier League")
  • Stadium (default: Anfield)
  • Match date/time
  • Predictions close at (deadline — members cannot predict after this)

After creation, note the Match ID (also available in the match dropdown).

─── PHASE 2: MEMBERS SUBMIT PREDICTIONS ───
Before "Predictions close at", active members go to:
  Member App → Match Hub → [match] → Submit prediction

Each member submits ONCE per match (can update before deadline).

Prediction fields:
  • Predicted score (Liverpool vs opponent)
  • Winner (Liverpool win / Draw / Away win)
  • First Liverpool goalscorer (text)
  • Man of the Match (text)
  • Clean sheet? (yes/no)
  • Salah to score? (yes/no)
  • Both teams to score? (yes/no)

─── PHASE 3: GO LIVE (kick-off) ───
On match day, admin → Matches → Live match control:
  1. Select the match from dropdown
  2. Click "Start LIVE (kick-off alert)"
  3. ALL active members receive a KICK OFF notification + full-screen alert

─── PHASE 4: RECORD GOALS DURING THE MATCH ───
Still in Live match control:
  1. Enter scorer name (e.g. "Salah")
  2. Enter minute (e.g. 23)
  3. Click the big red "GOOOOAAAAAL!" button for Liverpool goals
     OR "Opponent scores" for away goals

What happens:
  • Score auto-increments
  • ALL connected members see a full-screen GOOOOAAAAAL overlay
    (with scorer name, minute, score) — on ANY page they are on
  • Notification saved in their alerts list

─── PHASE 5: ENTER FINAL RESULT ───
After the match ends, admin → Matches → Final result:
  • Liverpool score, Away score
  • Winner (HOME_WIN / DRAW / AWAY_WIN)
  • First Liverpool scorer (official result)
  • Man of the Match
  • Clean sheet result, Salah scored, both teams scored (yes/no)
  • Click "Save final result" → match status becomes FINISHED

─── PHASE 6: CALCULATE PREDICTION POINTS ───
Admin → Matches → Final result section:
  Click "Calculate prediction points"

This compares every member's prediction against the official result and
awards points (see Section 9). Also updates leaderboard, XP, coins,
achievements, and Hall of Fame.


================================================================================
9. POINTS CALCULATION — DETAILED RULES
================================================================================

When you click "Calculate prediction points", the system checks each
member's prediction against the official final result.

DEFAULT POINTS (per match, per member):
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Correct prediction type              Points
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Correct winner (Liverpool/Draw/Away)   5 pts
  Exact final score                     10 pts
  Correct first Liverpool goalscorer     5 pts
  Correct Man of the Match               3 pts
  Correct clean sheet (yes/no)           2 pts
  Correct Salah to score (yes/no)        2 pts
  Correct both teams to score (yes/no)   2 pts
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  MAXIMUM possible per match            29 pts
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

EXAMPLE:
  Official result: Liverpool 2–1 Arsenal
  First scorer: Salah | MOTM: Salah | Clean sheet: No | Salah scored: Yes
  Both teams scored: Yes

  Member predicted: Liverpool 2–1, Salah scorer, Salah MOTM, no clean sheet,
  Salah yes, BTTS yes
  → Winner ✓ (5) + Exact score ✓ (10) + Scorer ✓ (5) + MOTM ✓ (3)
    + Clean sheet ✓ (2) + Salah ✓ (2) + BTTS ✓ (2) = 29 points

  Member predicted: Liverpool 1–0, wrong scorer, wrong MOTM
  → Winner ✓ (5) + Exact score ✗ + Scorer ✗ + MOTM ✗ + bonuses = 5–9 pts

Text fields (scorer, MOTM) are compared case-insensitively.

ACCURACY RATE (shown on leaderboard):
  accuracy = (total points earned) / (number of predictions × 10) × 100

STREAK:
  If a member gets the winner correct, their winning streak +1.
  If wrong, streak resets to 0.
  (Streak bonus points in scoring rules exist but are not active yet.)

10. GAMIFICATION — XP, COINS, LEVELS, ACHIEVEMENTS
================================================================================

Applied automatically when prediction points are calculated.

XP (Experience Points):
  • Base: 10 XP per scored prediction
  • Bonus: floor(total points / 2)
  • Extra +10 if exact score correct
  • Extra +5 if winner correct

Coins:
  • Base: 5 coins per scored prediction
  • Bonus: floor(total points / 3)
  • Extra +3 if exact score correct

LEVELS:
  Level 1 — Rookie Red         (0 XP)
  Level 2 — Kop Contender      (100 XP)
  Level 3 — Anfield Analyst    (250 XP)
  Level 4 — Matchday Maestro   (500 XP)
  Level 5 — Kop Legend         (900 XP)

ACHIEVEMENTS:
  • KING OF THE KOP — #1 on overall leaderboard (only one holder at a time)
  • PREDICTION MASTER — submitted 10 or more predictions

Hall of Fame (/app/hall-of-fame) displays King of the Kop, top 10, and
recent achievements.

11. LIVE GOOOOAAAAAL ALERTS — HOW THEY WORK
================================================================================

When admin records a goal during a LIVE match:

FOR LIVERPOOL GOALS:
  • Full-screen red overlay: "GOOOOAAAAAL!"
  • Scorer name, minute, live score
  • LFC crest, confetti animation, YNWA message
  • Appears on ALL pages (public site, member app, admin) if member is logged in
  • Auto-dismisses after 12 seconds or tap to close

FOR OPPONENT GOALS:
  • Subdued overlay: "They scored…"
  • Still shows score update

FOR KICK-OFF:
  • "KICK OFF!" overlay when admin clicks Start LIVE

Members must be LOGGED IN and have an ACTIVE account to receive alerts.
Polling checks for new notifications every 3 seconds.

  7. npm run dev:api   (terminal 1)
  8. npm run dev:web   (terminal 2)
  9. Open http://localhost:5173

  Email:    admin@olsclebanon.com
  Password: ChangeMeNow123!  (or value of SEED_SUPER_ADMIN_PASSWORD in .env)

20. IMPORTANT NOTES & LIMITATIONS
================================================================================

• Official membership is ALWAYS managed by the committee — not self-service
  on the platform.

• Members must activate their account before they can log in or predict.

• Predictions lock at "Predictions close at" — no late submissions.

• Points are only calculated AFTER you enter the final result AND click
  "Calculate prediction points". They are NOT automatic at full time.

• GOOOOAAAAAL alerts only reach logged-in ACTIVE members.

• Gallery admin upload has no UI yet — use API or ask developer.

• Scoring rules (point values) can be changed via API but not yet in admin UI.
  Current defaults are listed in Section 9.

• Streak bonus points are defined but not applied in calculations yet.

• Leaderboard in Member App shows OVERALL ranking only (weekly/monthly exist
  in API but not shown in UI yet).

• Email invitations require SMTP configuration. Without SMTP, invitation links
  are shown in the admin API response (development mode only).
